### Executive Summary

Personal hygiene and absorbent intimate apparel have historically relied on single-use disposable pads containing superabsorbent polymer granules (SAP), wood pulp, and bleached synthetic plastics—generating millions of tons of non-biodegradable landfill waste annually.

As lead inventor on **[GB Patent 2605773 B](https://patents.google.com/patent/GB2605773B/en)**, I engineered a **washable, multi-layered knitted textile architecture** that achieves rapid liquid acquisition, high core fluid retention under compressive pressure, and zero-leak containment. By engineering the capillary physics directly into the yarn geometry and stitch structure, the textile delivers permanent, chemical-free absorbency that withstands 50+ household wash cycles.

---

### 1. The Engineering Challenge: The Fluid Management Paradox

* **The Strike-Through vs. Leakage Conflict:** Designing an effective absorbent textile requires four opposing fluid behaviors in a single ultra-thin garment gusset:
  1. *Immediate Acquisition:* Pulling fluid away from skin in under 2 seconds ("feels like dry").
  2. *Lateral Dispersal:* Distributing liquid evenly across the core to prevent localized saturation and pooling.
  3. *Pressure Retention:* Preventing liquid from squeezing back out against the skin when the user sits or moves ("rewet").
  4. *Breathable Containment:* Blocking liquid leakage entirely while remaining permeable to air and water vapor.
* **The Wash-Out Problem of Chemical Finishes:** Conventional absorbent apparel uses chemical wicking top-coatings. These surface treatments wash out after 15–20 laundry cycles, leaving garments feeling damp, prone to odor retention, and structurally degraded.
* **Thermal Discomfort & Crinkling:** Traditional waterproof barriers use thick, laminated thermoplastic polyurethane (TPU) films that trap heat, feel stiff, and make audible crinkling noises during movement.

---

### 2. Technical Innovation & Patented Architecture

To overcome chemical finish degradation, I developed a purely structural, yarn-engineered fluid transport matrix:

* **Differential Capillary Pressure Gradient:** Engineered a progressive denier profile from the skin layer to the interior absorption core. By using coarser filaments at the skin face and micro-denier capillary channels in the inner matrix, capillary pressure naturally drives fluid inward, keeping the skin-facing surface dry to the touch.
* **Integrated 3D Reservoir Matrix:** Engineered localized stitch tucks and multi-filament spacer geometry that create structural micro-cavities within the knit, maximizing fluid holding volume while keeping total fabric thickness under 2.5 mm.
* **Mechanical Permanence (Zero Chemical Wash-Out):** Because the wicking and absorption dynamics are governed by yarn cross-sections and physical loop mechanics rather than topical chemical finishes, the fabric retains **100% absorption performance after 50+ industrial wash cycles**.
* **Micro-Porous Vapor-Permeable Barrier:** Co-developed an ultra-thin, silent knitted liquid barrier that prevents strike-through under dynamic body pressure while permitting continuous airflow and moisture-vapor transmission (reducing heat rash and microbial proliferation).

### 4. Commercial Applications & Impact

* **Reusable Intimate Apparel:** Applied as the functional core for washable period-care underwear, postpartum recovery briefs, and light urinary incontinence garments (FemTech / Femography).
* **Environmental Impact:** Replaces an estimated 200–300 single-use disposable plastic pads per consumer annually.
* **Activewear Micro-Climate Zones:** Scaled into targeted sweat-absorption and anti-chafing zones for high-exertion athletic undergarments.

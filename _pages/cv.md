### <span id="summary">Executive Summary</span>

Accomplished **Textile R&D Leader and Material Engineer** with over 10 years of experience delivering commercially successful, sustainable, and high-performance material innovations for global apparel and medical brands. 

Expert in advanced 3D flatbed knitting (Stoll & Shima Seiki), performance fabric development, cost-efficient decentralized manufacturing models, and material quality optimization. Proven track record of leading cross-functional engineering teams, driving open innovation across global supply chains, and translating laboratory material science into scalable, retail-ready garments.

---

### <span id="experience">Work Experience</span>

#### **Deputy General Manager — Technology Entrepreneur & Textile Engineer**
**Twinery, Innovation by MAS** | Colombo, Sri Lanka  
*April 2025 – Present*

* Lead material white-space and trend analysis at the Material Center of Excellence to drive strategic MAS Holdings innovation roadmaps.
* Define and execute advanced manufacturing strategies aligned with future textile capabilities.
* Conduct structured R&D on adaptive textile materials (hydromorphic, shape-memory polymers, and phase-change materials), mapping smart material behaviors to everyday clothing.
* Engineered squat-proof, lightshade legging fabrics optimized for opacity and structural integrity under 60% multidirectional stretch.
* Developed advanced soft-good textile covering systems for commercial robotics applications for a leading global robotics brand.

#### **Manager — R&D in Flatbed Knitting Technology**
**Twinery, Innovation by MAS** | Colombo, Sri Lanka  
*November 2021 – April 2025*

* **onEKnit Platform Lead:** Spearheaded MAS Holdings' decentralized manufacturing and next S-curve knitting initiative across cross-functional engineering teams.
* **Cost Engineering:** Reduced material and process costs by **28%** through structural yarn optimization and lean manufacturing translation.
* **Medical & Recovery Wearables:** Led the textile engineering of an active pneumatic compression medical sleeve and vibration therapy knitted sleeves with seamlessly integrated motors.
* **Regenerated Fiber Optimization:** Resolved pilling bottlenecks in regenerated fibers using Siro compact spinning, elevating rating from 2 to 3.5.
* **Circularity Breakthrough:** Engineered a 100% recyclable mono-material 3D-knitted bra cup to eliminate non-recyclable polyurethane foam.
* **IP Generation:** Invented and filed multiple patent disclosures protecting MAS Holdings' intellectual property portfolio.

#### **Assistant Manager — R&D in Flatbed Knitting Technology**
**Twinery, Innovation by MAS** | Colombo, Sri Lanka  
*December 2019 – November 2021*

* Engineered functional knits with targeted moisture management, passive cooling, passive heating, and antimicrobial functionalities.
* Completed advanced WholeGarment programming and machine optimization training under Prof. Tilak Dias at Nottingham Trent University (UK) across Stoll (M1 Plus) and Shima Seiki (APEX3) systems.
* Co-developed fast-wicking, antimicrobial, and fluorine-free hydrophobic functional yarns with global suppliers.
* Engineered high-performance multilayered brief gussets for incontinence and menstruation support (FemTech).

#### **Executive — Smart Textiles**
**Twinery, Innovation by MAS** | Colombo, Sri Lanka  
*March 2016 – December 2019*

* Engineered conductive pathways, bus-bars, and encapsulation methods for smart wearable devices.
* Applied low-temperature plasma technology for surface activation and durable hydrophobic textile finishes.
* Contributed to the active knitted heating wearable platform recognized with multiple **CES 2017 Innovation Awards**.

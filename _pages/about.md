I am an **Innovative Textile Engineer and Technology Entrepreneur** with over 10 years of R&D experience bridging advanced material science, 3D flatbed knitting, and commercial product engineering. Currently, I serve as **Deputy General Manager – Technology Entrepreneur** at **Twinery (Innovation by MAS)**, leading material white-space strategy and commercializing next-generation textile technologies.

My focus is on solving high-barrier engineering challenges—transitioning novel e-textiles, medical knits, and sustainable material architectures from early-stage lab concepts into globally distributed consumer and healthcare products.

---

### <span class="section-badge"><i class="fa-solid fa-layer-group"></i></span> Core Areas of Expertise

* **Garment Engineering & Body Mapping:** Comprehensive apparel product development integrating anatomical body mapping, technical material selection, and functional prototyping from concept to commercial scale.
* **Advanced 3D Flatbed Knitting:** Full-garment engineering, zonal modulus control, and structural programming on Shima Seiki (APEX3) and Stoll (M1 Plus) platforms.
* **Smart & Electronic Textiles (E-Textiles):** Conductive pathways, embedded sensors/actuators, wireless power transmission, and active heating platforms.
* **Medical & Therapeutic Wearables:** Knitted pneumatic compression sleeves, vibration therapy systems, and respiratory interface components.
* **Sustainable & Circular Engineering:** End-to-end design and engineering of circularity-enabled textiles material/ structures developed for closed-loop recycling.


---

### <span class="section-badge"><i class="fa-solid fa-globe"></i></span> Commercial Impact & Brand Deployments

Over the past decade, I have led technical R&D and manufacturing translation across commercial lifestyle, med-tech, and performance platforms:

* **Victoria’s Secret & PINK:** Co-developed and commercialized the circular mono-material 3D-knitted bra cup to replace conventional PU foam, advancing sustainable apparel circularity (Patented).
* **Calvin Klein:** Technical development and flat-knit engineering for lifestyle and undergarment collections.
* **Therabody:** Partnered on the flat-knitted architecture for the vibration therapy wearable system.
* **ResMed:** Engineered precision flat-knitted medical soft-goods (headgear and interface components).
* **Advanced Soft-Goods:** Prototyped specialized 3D-knitted protective coverings and conformal textile structures for robotic and industrial applications.
